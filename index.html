Know, Love & Follow Jesus

We aim to Know, Love & Follow Jesus

Know

If God exists, how would we know? Christians believe God made himself known in Jesus of Nazareth. Through his life, death and resurrection we can know God, receive forgiveness and share new life.

These are extraordinary claims. We’d love to explain why we believe. Alpha is a relaxed space to ask big questions.

Explore Alpha

Love

Knowing God leads to love for God and others. As we open the Bible and gather on Sundays and in small groups, God’s Spirit grows a strong, transforming love that makes a real difference.

It’s the kind of love that empowered Jesus to give his life—and it shapes us today.

Join a Group

Follow

Following Jesus is a daily calling—speaking good news and living to honour him in our communities. It’s not always easier, but it’s a great adventure with God at work.

You’re invited to discover what it means to know, love and follow him.
